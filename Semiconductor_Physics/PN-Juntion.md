# PN Junction
A PN junction is formed by joining:

- p-type semiconductor  
- n-type semiconductor  

---

# Formation of PN Junction

When p and n regions are joined:

## Step 1: Diffusion

- electrons move from n → p  
- holes move from p → n  

---

## Step 2: Recombination

- electrons + holes recombine  
- region near junction loses carriers  

---

## Step 3: Depletion Region

- region with no free carriers  
- contains fixed ions  

---

## Step 4: Electric Field Formation

- electric field develops across junction  
- opposes further movement  

---

# Built-in Potential

Voltage developed across depletion region.

Prevents continuous diffusion of carriers.

---

# Biasing

## Forward Bias

- p connected to positive  
- n connected to negative  

### Result:
- depletion region decreases  
- current flows  

---

## Reverse Bias

- p connected to negative  
- n connected to positive  

### Result:
- depletion region increases  
- very small current flows  

---

# 8. Key Insights

- intrinsic → no doping  
- extrinsic → controlled conductivity  
- doping changes carrier concentration  
- PN junction creates electric field  
- depletion region is central concept  

---

| Concept | Key Idea |

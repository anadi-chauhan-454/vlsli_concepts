# 1. Inductor

An inductor is a **two-terminal passive device** that stores energy in a **magnetic field**.

It is usually a coil of wire.

---

## Key Property

Inductor **resists change in current**.

---

## Voltage-Current Relation

V = L (dI/dt)

Where:
- V = voltage
- L = inductance (Henry)
- dI/dt = rate of change of current

---

## Energy Stored

E = (1/2) L I²

---

## Behavior

| Condition | Behavior |
|----------|--------|
| DC steady state | acts like short circuit |
| changing current | opposes change |
| AC signals | offers reactance |

---

## Important Insight

- Capacitor resists change in **voltage**
- Inductor resists change in **current**

---

# 2. RLC Circuit

An RLC circuit contains:

- Resistor (R)
- Inductor (L)
- Capacitor (C)

---

# Types of RLC Circuits

- Series RLC
- Parallel RLC

---

# Basic Equation (Series RLC)

V = IR + L(dI/dt) + (1/C) ∫I dt

---

# 3. Resonance (Very Important)

Resonance occurs when:

Inductive reactance = Capacitive reactance

---

## Resonant Frequency

f₀ = 1 / (2π√(LC))

---

## At Resonance

- impedance is minimum (series circuit)
- current is maximum
- circuit oscillates efficiently

---

# 4. Behavior of RLC Circuit

| Condition | Behavior |
|----------|--------|
| low frequency | capacitor dominates |
| high frequency | inductor dominates |
| resonance | balanced behavior |

---

# 5. Applications

- filters (low-pass, high-pass, band-pass)
- oscillators
- tuning circuits
- signal processing

---

# Key Insights

- Inductor stores energy in magnetic field  
- Capacitor stores energy in electric field  
- RLC circuits control frequency behavior  
- Resonance is the most important concept  

---

# Summary

| Component | Stores Energy In | Resists Change In |
|----------|----------------|------------------|
| Capacitor | Electric field | Voltage |
| Inductor | Magnetic field | Current |
